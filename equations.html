Finance Formula Cheat Sheet 💖

Type a formula or ratio name below to filter instantly:

1. Financial Statements & Taxes

Total Assets (TA)
TA = Current Assets (CA) + Fixed Assets (FA)
Balance Sheet Identity
Total Assets = Total Liabilities + Equity
Net Working Capital (NWC)
NWC = Current Assets (CA) - Current Liabilities (CL)
EBIT
EBIT = Sales - COGS - Operating Expenses - Depreciation
EBT (Earnings Before Taxes)
EBT = EBIT - Interest | EBT = Net Income / (1 - Tax Rate)
Net Income & Retained Earnings
NI = EBT - Taxes | Transfer to RE = NI - Dividends
Average Tax Rate
Average Tax Rate = Taxes / Taxable Income

2. Liquidity & Turnover Ratios

Current Ratio (CR)
CR = Current Assets (CA) / Current Liabilities (CL)
Quick Ratio (QR)
QR = (CA - Inventory - Prepaid Expenses) / CL
Accounts Receivable Turnover (ART)
ART = Credit Sales / Accounts Receivable (AR)
Average Collection Period (ACP)
ACP = 365 / ART | AR / (Sales / 365)
Inventory Turnover Ratio (ITR)
ITR = Sales / Inventory (Days in Inventory = 365 / ITR)
Total Assets Turnover (TAT)
TAT = Sales / Total Assets (TA)

3. Debt, Profitability & Market Ratios

Debt Ratio & Debt-Equity Ratio
DR = Total Debt / TA | D/E = Total Debt / Equity
Equity Multiplier (EM)
EM = Total Assets / Equity = 1 + (D/E)
Net Profit Margin (NPM)
NPM = Net Income (NI) / Sales
Return on Assets (ROA) & Equity (ROE)
ROA = NI / TA | ROE = NI / Equity
DuPont Identity
ROE = NPM x TAT x EM
Earnings Per Share (EPS) & P/E Ratio
EPS = NI / Shares | P/E = Market Price per Share / EPS
Market-to-Book Ratio (MTB)
MTB = Market Value per Share / Book Value per Share

4. Interest Rates & Bonds

Bond Value
Bond Value = Coupon x [ (1 - (1 + r)^-t) / r ] + FV / (1 + r)^t
Current Yield
Current Yield = Annual Coupon / Today's Market Price
Call Premium & After-Tax Yield
Call Premium = Call Price - Par | After-Tax Yield = Taxable Yield x (1 - Tax Rate)
Fisher Effect
1 + Nominal Rate = (1 + Real Rate) x (1 + Inflation Rate)

5. Capital Budgeting

Net Present Value (NPV)
NPV = PV of Future Cash Flows + Initial Outlay (Accept if NPV > 0)
Profitability Index (PI)
PI = PV of Future Cash Flows / |Initial Outlay| (Accept if PI > 1)
IRR & MIRR
IRR: Rate where NPV = 0 | MIRR: Rate where PV Outflows = FV Inflows
